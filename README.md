### Frustum volume formula
**What is a frustrum?**
A frustrum is formed when the top of a pyramid or cone is cut off parallel to the base.  
The general formula for the volume of a frustum (of a cone or pyramid) is:  

$\(V = \frac{1}{3}h(A_1 + A_2 + \sqrt{A_1A_2})\)$
